Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This page explains what information I collect through this website and how it's used. I've kept this short and in plain language.

What I collect

  • Booking details: when you book a call through Calendly, Calendly collects your name, email, and any notes you add. See Calendly's own privacy policy for how they handle this.
  • Email correspondence: if you email me directly, I keep that correspondence for as long as needed to respond and manage our working relationship.
  • Payment information: if you enroll in the Program, payment is handled by a third-party payment processor. I never see or store your full card details.

What I don't do

I don't sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. I don't use tracking cookies or analytics scripts on this site beyond what Calendly's booking widget itself requires to function.

How long I keep it

I keep client records (session notes, correspondence) for as long as reasonably needed to deliver the Program and meet any legal or accounting obligations, and delete them on request where I'm not required to retain them.

Your rights

If you're in the EU/EEA, you have rights under GDPR to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. To exercise any of these, email tumer.ferruh@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach me at tumer.ferruh@gmail.com.
