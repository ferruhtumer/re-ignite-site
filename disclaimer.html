Coaching Disclaimer

Last updated: August 2026

Please read this before enrolling in Re-Ignite. It explains what coaching is, and — just as importantly — what it isn't.

Coaching is not therapy or medical treatment

I am a Certified Coach, not a licensed therapist, psychologist, psychiatrist, or medical professional. Coaching is future-focused and action-oriented; it is not a substitute for mental health treatment, diagnosis, or crisis support. If you're experiencing a mental health crisis, please contact a licensed professional or your local emergency services rather than relying on coaching.

No guaranteed outcomes

Coaching results depend heavily on your own engagement, honesty, and follow-through between sessions. I make no promises about specific career, financial, or personal outcomes.

You're responsible for your own decisions

I'll challenge your thinking, ask direct questions, and offer perspective — but any decisions you make during or after coaching (about your job, relationships, finances, or anything else) are yours alone. I'm not liable for the outcomes of decisions you choose to make.

Confidential, not privileged

I treat everything you share as confidential (see the Terms & Conditions), but coaching conversations do not carry the same legal privilege as conversations with a lawyer or licensed therapist.

Questions

If anything here is unclear before you book, ask me directly at tumer.ferruh@gmail.com — happy to talk it through.
